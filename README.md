# Hi, I'm Emmanuel Idoko (Pidoxy) 👋

Software engineer and AI researcher. I research medical imaging and reliable AI, build software at HabariPay (GTCO), and founded [AidCare](https://aidcare.pidoxy.com), a clinical decision-support platform for frontline care. I also teach AI for Software Engineering at The AI Colony.

## Research

- **When Retrieval Hurts**: retrieval-induced hallucination in chest X-ray report generation. Africa at NeurIPS 2026 (accepted, first author) · [paper page](https://www.pidoxy.com/research/when-retrieval-hurts) · baseline pipeline in [RGCA](https://github.com/pidoxy/RGCA)
- **VAMAE**: vessel-aware masked autoencoders for OCT angiography. ICPR 2026 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.06583)
- **SharpXR**: structure-aware denoising for pediatric chest X-rays. MIRASOL Workshop, MICCAI 2025 · [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.08518) · [code](https://github.com/ileri-oluwa-kiiye/SharpXR)
